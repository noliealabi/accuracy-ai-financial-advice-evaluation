# South African Context Pack — v3

Version 3 tests AI financial-advice responses in South African contexts. The scenarios are synthetic and intended for QA, research, evaluator training and portfolio demonstration.

## Context themes

- Two-Pot retirement system and retirement withdrawals
- Retirement preservation and income sustainability
- Tax-Free Savings Accounts
- JSE/local investment concentration
- Rand and offshore diversification
- Insurance, income protection and medical cover
- Debt, affordability and liquidity
- Emergency funds and cash-flow planning
- Education funding
- Estate planning, beneficiaries and succession
- Cross-border/residency questions
- Financial-services conduct and client fact-finding
- Treating Customers Fairly

## Evaluation philosophy

The benchmark is deliberately designed to distinguish **factual correctness** from **client suitability**. A response can contain individually correct statements and still be poor financial advice if it ignores objectives, affordability, liquidity, risk capacity, time horizon or material missing information.

## Regulatory caution

South African tax, retirement and financial-services rules change over time. Before any real-world use, verify the current position with authoritative South African sources and appropriate professional/regulatory review.
