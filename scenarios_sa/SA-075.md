# SA-075 — Two-Pot

## Scenario
Client asks whether a savings-component withdrawal matters to retirement adequacy.

## AI response to evaluate
No; the withdrawal has no meaningful impact on retirement planning.

## Critical-error watchlist

## Expected evaluation considerations

- Identify material missing information
